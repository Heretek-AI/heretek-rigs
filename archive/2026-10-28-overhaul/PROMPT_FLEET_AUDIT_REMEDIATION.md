## 2. Hard Rules of Remediation

1. **Fix the Canonical Catalog & Generator, Not Just Generated Leaves**:
   - `fleet/fleet.yaml` and `fleet/mcp-servers.yaml` are the single canonical YAML source of truth; everything under `specs/agents/`, `rigs/`, `rosters/`, and `registry/` is deterministically generated by `scripts/generate-rigs.py` (using `scripts/render-seat.py` for per-role Pi runtime configs).
   - If a finding identifies a defect in `rig.yaml`, `agent.yaml`, `configurations.yaml`, `behaviour.json`, `status.json`, `bundle.yaml`, `guidance/role.md`, `runtime/mcp.json`, `runtime/policy-enforcement.json`, or `runtime/pi-permission-config.json`, fix the root cause in `fleet/fleet.yaml`, `fleet/mcp-servers.yaml`, `scripts/generate-rigs.py`, or `scripts/render-seat.py` AND re-run `python3 scripts/generate-rigs.py` so the fix persists across regenerations and all cryptographic digests (`configurationId`, `packageDigest`, `bodyDigest`) stay synchronized.
2. **Strengthen `scripts/verify-rigs.sh` on Any Survived Sabotage**:
   - If the audit's Sabotage Results table shows that any `[S]` invariant survived sabotage (`Caught? = No`), add an explicit, fail-closed check to `scripts/verify-rigs.sh` and verify that breaking the invariant in a scratch copy causes `./scripts/verify-rigs.sh --check-only` to exit 1.
3. **Never Weaken Security, Permission Guards, or Schema Validation**:
   - Never delete or skip a check in `scripts/verify-rigs.sh` or `scripts/audit_loop.py`.
   - Never weaken `permission_policy: builtin:locked` on the 18 locked seats (the 16 read-only-charter roles plus `sabotage-qa-tester` and `static-analysis-lead`).
   - Never remove `"*..*": "deny"` as the final key in `path_write` and `path` in `extensions/pi-permission-system/config.json`.
   - Never remove `"code_rewrite": "deny"`, `"lsp_rename": "deny"`, or `"lsp_code_actions": "deny"` from `extensions/pi-permission-system/config.json`.
   - Never introduce `guidance_merge` into `agent.yaml` `startup.files` (always provision role charters to per-seat `<agentDir>/AGENTS.md` to avoid shared `<cwd>/AGENTS.md` overwrites).
   - Never replace command-backed `"!cat /abs/path/<key>"` secret templates with literal secrets or place secrets/`OPENRIG_*` variables into `rig.yaml` `env:`.
   - Never enable `POST`, `BUILD`, `EXEC`, `VOLUMES`, `NETWORKS`, or `AUTH` on `docker-socket-proxy`, never bind a sidecar host port to `0.0.0.0`, and never introduce a `:latest` tag or symlink inside `specs/` or `rigs/`.
4. **Stop After 3 Failed Attempts on the Same Error**:
   - If the same fix fails verification 3 times, record the exact commands and outputs in `docs/NOTES.md` and `REMEDIATION_LOG.md` rather than guessing blindly.
