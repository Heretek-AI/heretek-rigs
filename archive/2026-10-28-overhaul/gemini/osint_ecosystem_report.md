This OSINT report documents the ecosystem discovery, security/token-efficiency rubric evaluation, and role-by-role mapping required to migrate the entire specialized agent fleet from [Heretek-Crew](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2FHeretek-AI%2FHeretek-Crew) to a greenfield, pod-aware [OpenRig](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2Fmvschwarz%2Fopenrig) monorepo ([heretek-rigs](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2FHeretek-AI%2Fheretek-rigs)).

Direct inspection of `roles-catalog/ROLE_CATALOG_50.md`, `scripts/generate-50-roles.py`, and `roles/*` in [Heretek-Crew](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2FHeretek-AI%2FHeretek-Crew) proves that the production fleet actually comprises **51 canonical specialist roles** across 9 departments (totaling $13,000.00/month in paperclip budget caps):
- Roles `01` through `50` are joined by **Role `51` (`ai-red-teamer`)** in `roles-catalog/07_defensive_security_and_forensics.md` (promoted in Wave C P1-4).
- In the initial 7-rig topology draft, `ai-red-teamer` was placed into Rig 7 (`defensive-sec-and-red-team`), while **Role `37` (`static-analysis-lead`)** from Department 6 (`roles-catalog/06_quality_assurance_and_verification.md`) was inadvertently omitted from Rig 6 (`qa-and-verification-gauntlet`).
- To guarantee **100% zero-loss parity** with `ROLE_CATALOG_50.md` and `Heretek-Crew/roles/*`, `heretek-rigs` restores `static-analysis-lead` into Rig 6 (`qa-and-verification-gauntlet`, `gate-review` pod) alongside `release-qa-officer`, bringing the total across Rigs 1–7 (and Rig 8 `heretek-omni-fleet`) to all **51 specialist roles**.

## 2. 4-Pillar OSINT Evaluation Rubric

Every candidate Pi package, `SKILL.md` module, and Model Context Protocol (MCP) server evaluated for `heretek-rigs` was scored across four mandatory architectural pillars:

| Pillar | Weight | Evaluation Criteria | Fail-Closed Rejection Trigger |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Maintenance & Provenance** | 25% | Active 2026 commits, verified npm/PyPI/GitHub releases, real TypeScript/Rust/Go/Python implementation, clean OSI license (MIT/Apache-2.0/BSD). | Archived repo, unresolvable registry version, or hollow wrapper around unpinned shell strings. |
| **2. OpenRig & Pi Ergonomics** | 25% | Native compatibility with `pi --mode rpc`, `$PI_CODING_AGENT_DIR` (`~/.openrig/state/pi/<session>/agent`), `agent.yaml` (v1.0), `rig.yaml` (v0.2), and non-interactive execution. | Requires interactive TTY prompts during headless RPC turns or mutates global host state outside `$OPENRIG_HOME`. |
| **3. Token Efficiency** | 25% | Lazy tool schema loading, compact output truncation, and progressive-disclosure `SKILL.md` references that preserve [Muse Spark 1.3 Contributor](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fopencode.ai%2Fdata%2Fmeta%2Fmuse-spark-1-3-contributor)'s 1,048,576-token context window and 95% prompt-cache hit ratio. | Dumps >10,000 tokens of raw tool schemas into every turn startup or breaks prefix prompt caching via mid-session schema churn. |
| **4. Security & Blast Radius** | 25% | Strict separation of read-only vs. mutating verbs, explicit `argv` array execution (no shell interpolation), SSRF guards, and alignment with OpenRig permission policies (`builtin:locked`, `builtin:standard`, `builtin:auto`). | Unscoped shell/filesystem write primitives on read-only auditor seats or unauthenticated remote execution exposure. |
